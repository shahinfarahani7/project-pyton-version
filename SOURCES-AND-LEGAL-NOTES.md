# Sources and Legal Notes

External standards and policies are volatile. Before release, re-check official sources and store dated copies in the compliance evidence system.

- OpenAPI Specification: https://spec.openapis.org/oas/latest.html
- AsyncAPI Specification: https://www.asyncapi.com/docs/reference/specification/latest
- CloudEvents: https://cloudevents.io/
- JSON Schema 2020-12: https://json-schema.org/draft/2020-12
- OWASP ASVS: https://owasp.org/www-project-application-security-verification-standard/
- Apple App Review Guidelines: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- Google Play developer policies: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/
- EU Markets in Crypto-assets Regulation: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1114/oj/eng
- Finnish Financial Supervisory Authority crypto-asset activities: https://www.finanssivalvonta.fi/en/financial-market-participants/capital-markets/crypto-asset-activities/

This pack is an engineering baseline, not legal, tax, accounting or investment advice. Token and payout features remain disabled until jurisdiction-specific written approval.
