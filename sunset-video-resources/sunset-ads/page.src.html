参T-shirt

The shirt is the night sky

The back print is cut to a real ukiyo-e ōban sheet. The sunset steps down in flat bands of ink the way a woodblock print lays its colours, and the top is left unprinted, so the indigo shirt becomes the night with a crescent moon: today’s sun under tonight’s sky. The sea is made of the mark’s own waves. A title slip reads 今日の陽を今夜に, sealed with 陽.

How these were made

Artwork

Every piece is HTML set in physical millimetres with the site’s fonts, colours, mark geometry and 陽 seal, and rendered headlessly at print resolution. The sunset is the site’s own woodblock shader, re-rendered in tiles at print size, without the glitter path. Scenes come from the site’s 3D build.

In place

The environments were generated with Aura with blank surfaces. Each ad is the rendered PNG, resampled once through the surface’s homography and lit by the blank surface itself: the board’s lamp pools, the lightbox falloff, the fabric’s folds, the card’s light. The ads are never redrawn. The platform’s lightbox frame was extended along the wall plane to the panel’s true 3.54:1.
