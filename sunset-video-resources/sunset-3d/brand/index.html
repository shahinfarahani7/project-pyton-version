Sunset · brand · logo exploration

A setting sun,
cut into panel rows.

10 vector directions (mark, real-type “Sunset” lockup and a heavier 16 px favicon cut, each in light and dark) and 6 Higgsfield emblem directions keyed to transparency. Every one is shown large, in the page’s nav pill with a 26 px mark, and as a 16 px browser-tab favicon.

Recommendation

The pick, refined

Dusk cell, refined from S02 Cell sun: the disc now sets — cut at 62% of its radius below the centre by a horizon rule whose centre sits on the wordmark baseline, so the sun goes down behind the word. Three columns × three rows are the page's cell grid; the rows warm toward vermilion as it sets (on paper they start at sun-2 #F08A4B because gold is only 1.6:1 there; on night they start at gold). The 16 px favicon is a separate cut with 4.3/48 gaps so the grid still shows in a browser tab.

  1. #1 · Dusk cellThe only direction that says solar, sunset and the page's hairline grid in one silhouette. Ownable (not a ray fan, not a plain disc), survives 16 px as a tiled sun on a line, and shifts its palette per theme rather than just inverting.
  2. #2 · Shu-in sealMost Japanese and the strongest at 16 px — a vermilion chop with the sun and the sea carved out reads instantly in a tab and on both grounds. Less specific to solar; best kept as the secondary stamp (hanko on documents, stickers, the plan contract).
  3. #3 · Yū 夕The most distinctive: the character for evening carved into the sun, in real Shippori Mincho. Bilingual and elegant at 26 px and up, but at 16 px it collapses to a red dot that reads as the Hinomaru, and it says nothing about panels.
Vector · hand-built SVG

SVG directions

Marks are drawn on a 48-unit grid. The wordmark is Instrument Serif outlined with HarfBuzz + fontTools (its kerning kept, −8/1000 tracking); 夕 is Shippori Mincho, outlined. In the lockup the mark is 1.42 × the cap height, the gap 0.46 × cap, optically centred on the caps — the proportions of the live nav (26 px mark, 25 px serif). Each favicon is a separate, heavier cut for 16 px.

Raster · Higgsfield, keyed to alpha

PNG emblem directions

Generated on pure black with “no text, no letters, no characters”, then keyed: alpha from the max channel above a knee of ≈ 20, colours un-premultiplied for soft edges, frame edges cropped, holes filled for solid emblems. Single-colour cream emblems get an ink recolour for paper. The “Sunset” beside each is the outlined Instrument Serif wordmark, never model-drawn type.

Notes

References & method

  • Daylight — footer lockup + nav — half-sun mark ≈ cap height, sitting on the baseline, gap ≈ 0.35 cap; we align Dusk cell's horizon to the baseline the same way, and avoid a ray fan (Daylight owns it).
  • Daylight — nav pill over a hairline grid — almost our page: the nav mark is ~16 px, so anything under ~1.5 px disappears — hence separate favicon cuts.
  • Daylight — large lockup in the wild — one lockup scaled uniformly from nav to billboard; proportions never change.
  • Structured — serif wordmark, circled monogram — a serif wordmark carries the brand; a contained mark (circle / seal) survives small sizes.
  • Garden — hairline mark + serif — premium at large sizes, but hairlines die at 16 px; our marks keep gaps ≥ 3/48.
  • Wispr Flow — mark at the wordmark's height — mark and word at the same optical height, gap ≈ ½ a letter; our lockups use mark 1.42 × cap (the mark has no x-height to lean on).
  • T1 Energy — solar nav — a heavy geometric mark reads over photography; favicon is the mark in a solid tile, like the Shu-in seal.
  • Sana — mark and wordmark split across the nav — the mark has to stand alone, so every direction ships a mark-only file.
  • Headspace — plain orange disc — a bare sun disc is generic (and in Japan a red disc reads as the Hinomaru), so every sun here is cut: by a grid, a horizon, or 夕.

Higgsfield spend: 2526.7 → 2517.8 credits = 8.9 credits over 15 jobs (11 Z Image at 0.15 — 6 completed, 5 failed and refunded; 4 Nano Banana Pro at 2k, 2 credits each). The quoted costs matched the charges. At an assumed $0.06 per credit that is ≈ $0.53, under the $1 cap. Keying: alpha from max(R,G,B) above a knee of 20–55 (the Z Image blacks are not pure: 11–44), un-premultiplied colour, hole fill for solid emblems, a circle mask and a saturation floor to drop the lacquer-rim glint on the gold.
