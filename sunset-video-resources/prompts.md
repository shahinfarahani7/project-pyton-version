# Sunset workflow prompts

These reusable prompts are edited from the saved captions of `Opus5-5-Mobbin.cut`. They preserve the demonstrated requests, but are not verbatim exports of the original chat. The timestamps refer to the saved 47:49 timeline. The separate full scene prompts mentioned near 38 minutes still need their exact source-post URLs.

## 1. Find references (00:08:47–00:09:47)

```text
Give me five websites from Mobbin that could inspire a solar-panel website. Show screenshots as well as links, and explain which sections would be useful for this project.
```

## 2. Plan the sections (00:10:50–00:12:23)

```text
Use Daylight as one reference for the landing page and map, Origin for charts, and other relevant websites for testimonials and pricing. Combine the useful ideas into a plan for five to ten sections. Show the screenshots and explain the section order before building.
```

## 3. Define the art direction (00:14:26–00:16:32)

```text
The brand is Sunset, a solar and home-battery concept for Japan. Build a clean Three.js landing page with Japanese architecture, sakura trees, falling petals and colors around sunset.

Use a floating menu. Carry the lines and grids of solar panels through the design. Make the house, solar panels, phone and charts in 3D. Start with a light beige background and alternate with darker sections. Keep every section part of the same visual direction while adapting the references.

Self-verify until it's perfect.
```

## 4. Choose assets deliberately (00:17:34–00:19:41)

```text
Use 3D for buildings, phones, landscapes and backgrounds. Generate images of people with Higgsfield or Aura instead of building them from primitive geometry.

Use transparent PNG layers for Japanese ornaments, stamps and branches. Use Iconify Solar Duotone icons instead of drawing the icons from scratch.
```

## 5. Improve materials and lighting (00:19:41–00:21:29)

```text
Improve the textures, geometry and lighting in every 3D scene for a Retina display. Wood should look like wood. Add convincing light from the background and check the details at full size.

Score each scene out of ten against the references. Identify its weakest parts, improve them, and verify again. Aim for at least eight out of ten within the agreed budget.
```

## 6. Add specific interactions (00:26:23–00:28:58)

```text
Let the 3D objects orbit slightly on hover and rotate on drag. Add pointer-driven ripples to the water in the footer. Keep the sunset theme in the shader buttons.

Separate the couple and the background into different image layers. Add parallax between them as the page scrolls.
```

## 7. Explore the identity (00:31:02–00:34:11)

```text
Explore logo directions for Sunset in a separate task. Compare an SVG direction with transparent PNG directions generated through Higgsfield. Use the existing website and relevant Mobbin references.

Create a separate brand guide from this website. Include the logo, colors, typography, hierarchy, imagery and reusable assets.
```

## 8. Build the advertising set (00:34:11–00:35:46)

```text
Create advertising concepts for this product using the existing Sunset website and brand direction. Include billboard, business card, T-shirt and subway placements. Keep this work in a separate task.

Before any paid image generation, show the estimated total and ask if the cumulative spend would exceed $1. Do not generate more than ten images without asking.
```
