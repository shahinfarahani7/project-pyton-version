# Daily and Monthly Finance Close

Daily: reconcile payment settlements, customer balance, reservations, task charges/releases, worker accruals, claim batches and ledger balance. Unresolved items create cases and block irreversible settlement where appropriate.

Monthly: lock PriceBook/RewardPolicy snapshots, recognize subscription/usage revenue, calculate tax and processor fees, age liabilities, review promotion budget, worker payable and token treasury coverage, issue invoices and preserve signed close evidence.
