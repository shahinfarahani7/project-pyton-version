# Worker Terms of Service (GA1)

Effective version: `worker-terms-v2.1.0` (2026-07-20)

## Participation model
Workers opt in through consent for terms, privacy, resource use, and reward disclosure. Assignment is server-side auto-lease; per-task accept/reject is not offered.

## Rewards and payouts
- Rewards accrue as EUR micro-unit liabilities, not tokens or guaranteed earnings.
- Payouts require completed KYC, sanctions screening, and supported-country eligibility via Stripe Connect Express.
- Unsupported countries fail closed; earning is disabled without manual bypass.

## Resource use
Workers control availability, network, charging, battery, and schedule preferences. Active leases continue under policy until completion, safe checkpoint, or server reassignment.

## Labor classification
Participation is structured as independent task execution with transparent reward formulas; no employment relationship is created by platform copy alone. Country-specific labor analysis is recorded in the compliance evidence bundle.
