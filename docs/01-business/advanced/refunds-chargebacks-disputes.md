# Refunds, Chargebacks and Disputes

- A refund references original usage and ledger transactions; it never edits them.
- A card chargeback affects payment settlement and customer balance risk, not historical task usage.
- A result-quality dispute freezes the disputed revenue/reward portion according to policy but preserves evidence.
- Duplicate charges are automatically refundable after idempotency/reconciliation proof.
- Service credits are contractual credits and are recorded separately from cash refunds.
- Every manual adjustment requires reason code, evidence, approver and immutable audit event.
